**Cueline 1.0.0: captions from speech in Premiere Pro, on your computer.**

### What it does
- **Transcribe and place:** turns the speech in a sequence (or its In/Out) into captions on a caption track. Hebrew first, with ivrit.ai's model, and 99 other languages with Whisper.
- **Timing from the audio:** captions start and end where the speech does, not where the model guessed.
- **Review:** every caption checked against the style (reading speed, length, lines), fixed automatically where it can be, the rest one click away. Waveform, playback in Premiere Pro, undo.
- **Speakers:** told apart by voice, or by audio track. New caption when someone else talks, dialogue dashes, a caption track per speaker, rename and correct by hand, unsure ones flagged.
- **Batch:** several sequences, one after another.

### Updating
Download the installer below and run it, then restart Premiere Pro. Cueline tells you when a new version is out (Updates in its menu).

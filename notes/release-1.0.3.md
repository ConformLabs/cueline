**Cueline Captions 1.0.3: the new name.**

### Changed
- **Cueline is now Cueline Captions**, like Surehand MCP: in Premiere Pro's **Window › Extensions › Cueline Captions**, the panel, About and help, the installers and Windows' Apps list. Your models, settings and history carry over; nothing else changes.

### Updating
Download the installer below and run it, then restart Premiere Pro. The panel is now under **Window › Extensions › Cueline Captions**. Cueline tells you when a new version is out (Updates in its menu).

Tested on Premiere Pro 2026 (26.5) on macOS 26 (Apple silicon) and Windows 11 (RTX 5070 Ti with Vulkan).

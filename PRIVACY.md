# Privacy

Cueline Captions is made by Conform Labs. It has no accounts, no analytics and no telemetry. Your audio, transcripts and
captions stay on your computer: transcription and speaker detection run locally.

Cueline goes online only for these, each from the Cueline panel:

| What | Where | When |
|---|---|---|
| Speech models | huggingface.co (ivrit.ai, ggml-org) | When you download a model in Models |
| Silence model | huggingface.co (ggml-org/whisper-vad) | Once, on the first transcription |
| Speaker models | huggingface.co and github.com (k2-fsa/sherpa-onnx) | Once, on the first run with speakers by voice |
| Update check | api.github.com (ConformLabs/cueline releases) | At most once a day; off in Updates › Check automatically |

Links in About and help open in your browser.

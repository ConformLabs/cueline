# Caption style and glossary

## Styles

| Style | Characters per line | Lines | Reading speed (max) | Shortest | Longest |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Broadcast | 42 | 2 | 17 characters/s | 1.0 s | 7.0 s |
| Social | 24 | 1 | 20 characters/s | 0.6 s | 3.0 s |
| One word | one word per caption | 1 | | 0.2 s | 2.0 s |

**☰ › Caption style and glossary** has a Custom style with every number editable, and the gap between captions.

Line breaks follow subtitle rules: a line never ends on a short connecting word (את, של, על, the, of, to…), lines
break at commas and before a new clause, and a number stays with the word after it.

For Hebrew and Arabic, **RTL fixed** adds the direction marks Premiere Pro needs so punctuation lands on the right side.

## Glossary

Names and terms Whisper gets wrong: write what it hears and what it should be ("תל-אביב" → "תל אביב"). Cueline
replaces them in the captions and also gives the right spellings to Whisper as a hint.

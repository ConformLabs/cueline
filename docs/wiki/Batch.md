# Batch

**Transcribe several sequences** (under Transcribe, or ☰ › Work) lists every sequence in the project with its length
and whether it has In/Out. Sequences with audio are picked; pick the ones you want.

Cueline opens each one in Premiere Pro and transcribes it with the main screen's settings (language, model, style,
speakers), one after another, and places its captions on a caption track. **Stop** ends the batch after the current
sequence.

At the end, a list shows each sequence's captions, how many to review, speakers and whether they were placed.
**Review** opens that sequence and its captions; **Back** returns to the list.

# Animated captions

Besides a caption track, Cueline can place your captions as animated graphics: one graphic per caption on a new video
track, with each word lit in its speaker's colour as it is spoken. It is the look of social videos, and everything
about it can be changed in Premiere Pro's Essential Graphics panel.

## Placing them

1. Transcribe and review as usual.
2. In the review screen, open **More (…) › Animated captions on a video track**.
3. Pick a look and click **Place**. The graphics go on a new video track above everything else, so nothing in your edit
   is covered or replaced. Hebrew and Arabic read right to left.

Cueline positions them for the sequence: low in a landscape frame, a little below the middle of a portrait one. On
frames larger than HD the graphics are scaled up to match.

## Looks

| Look | What it does |
|---|---|
| Clean | White text, the spoken word in colour, a soft shadow. |
| Bold pop | Capitals with a thick outline; the spoken word bounces. |
| Box karaoke | A coloured box moves from word to word. |
| Dark pill | A rounded dark box behind the whole caption. |
| Word by word | Words appear as they are spoken. |
| Karaoke fill | Words fill with colour and stay lit. |
| Neon | A glow in the speaker's colour. |
| Social tilt | Capitals, a slight tilt per caption, a bouncy entrance. |
| Plain subtitles | Classic subtitles in a soft box, no highlight. |

## Changing the look

Select a graphic and open **Window › Essential Graphics**. The controls are grouped by name:

- **Caption:** the words. Edit Properties gives you the font, font size and bold/italic.
- **Text:** colour, case (Normal or UPPERCASE), letter spacing, line spacing, outline width and colour, size,
  position. Size and Line spacing are 50 at normal (Premiere Pro keeps these sliders between 0 and 100).
- **Spoken word:** a colour per speaker, the style (coloured word, coloured box, or coloured word on a box), how much it
  grows, and its animation (pop, bounce, fade in, rise). The box has its own colour, text colour, padding and roundness.
- **Said words:** go back to the text colour, keep the highlight, or take their own colour.
- **Upcoming words:** opacity. At 0, words appear one by one.
- **Background:** a box behind the caption, with colour, opacity, padding and roundness.
- **Shadow:** colour, opacity, distance and softness.
- **Caption entrance:** none, pop, fade, slide up or bounce. **Random tilt** turns each caption slightly.

Two fields at the bottom, **Word timing** and **Word speakers**, are set by Cueline. Leave them as they are.

## The same look on every caption

Style one caption, select it on the timeline, then in Cueline's **Animated captions** screen click **Copy the selected
caption's look to all**. Every other caption on that track takes its look and font. Their words and timing stay theirs.

If you change the font, copy the look afterwards even when you only styled one caption: Premiere Pro draws a changed
font itself, and copying is how the box behind the spoken word learns how wide the new font is.

## Versions

The templates open in Premiere Pro 2025 and later. They are After Effects templates, but After Effects doesn't need to
be installed.
